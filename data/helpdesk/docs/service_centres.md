# Service Centres and Charges

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## Service hotline

The hotline 1919-PARAVI is open from 8 am to 8 pm every day, including public holidays except Sinhala and Tamil New Year day.

## Service centres

- Colombo (Kirulapone): Monday to Saturday, 8.30 am to 5.30 pm.
- Kandy (Peradeniya Road): Monday to Saturday, 8.30 am to 5 pm.
- Galle (Wakwella Road): Monday to Friday, 8.30 am to 5 pm.
- Jaffna (Hospital Road): Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm.

## Charges outside warranty

A home visit outside the warranty period costs LKR 2,500, which is waived if the customer goes ahead with the repair. Gas refilling for an air conditioner costs LKR 9,000. Spare parts are charged at the listed price, with a 6-month warranty on the part fitted.

## Annual service for air conditioners

An annual service, which includes a full indoor and outdoor clean and a gas pressure check, costs LKR 4,500 per unit. It is recommended once a year, or twice a year in dusty areas.
