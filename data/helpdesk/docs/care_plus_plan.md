# Care+ Extended Warranty Plan

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## What Care+ adds

Care+ extends the full parts-and-labour warranty from 2 years to 5 years. It also includes one free annual service each year for air conditioners and priority technician visits within 2 working days anywhere in the country.

## Price

Care+ costs 8 percent of the appliance price for refrigerators and washing machines, and 10 percent for air conditioners.

## When to buy

Care+ must be bought within 30 days of the invoice date. It cannot be added later, and it cannot be bought for an appliance that has already had a repair.

## Transfer

Care+ stays with the appliance if it is sold, provided the new owner registers the transfer with the hotline within 30 days.
