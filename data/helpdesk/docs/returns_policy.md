# Paravi Returns and Refunds

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## Change of mind

An appliance may be returned within 7 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original, unopened packaging. A refund is made to the original payment method within 10 working days of collection. Delivery charges are not refunded.

## Dead on arrival

If an appliance does not work when first switched on, report it within 14 days of delivery. After a technician confirms the fault, the appliance is replaced with a new unit. Replacements are not made for faults caused by incorrect installation.

## Opened items

Opened items are not accepted for change-of-mind returns. Faults in opened items are handled under the warranty policy.

## Instalment purchases

For purchases on an instalment plan, a return cancels the plan; instalments already paid are refunded, less any interest charged by the bank.
