# Paravi PX-12 Inverter Air Conditioner — Owner's Manual (extract)

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## Remote control modes

COOL lowers the room temperature to the set point. DRY removes moisture with the fan at low speed and is best for humid evenings when the room is not hot. FAN circulates air without cooling. SLEEP raises the set point by 1 °C after one hour and by another 1 °C after two hours, so the room does not become cold during the night.

## Cleaning the filters

Open the front panel and slide out the two mesh filters. Wash them in lukewarm water with mild soap, dry them in the shade and refit them. Clean the filters every two weeks in normal use. A blocked filter reduces cooling and raises the electricity bill by up to 15 percent.

## Error codes

- E1: Indoor temperature sensor fault. Switch off at the mains for 5 minutes. If E1 returns, call service.
- E3: Outdoor fan motor fault. Check that nothing is blocking the outdoor fan.
- E5: Low refrigerant or a gas leak. Do not keep restarting the unit; call service.
- E6: Communication fault between indoor and outdoor units, usually a loose cable after installation or a power surge.
- P4: Voltage protection. Supply voltage is below 180 V or above 260 V. The unit restarts by itself when the voltage returns to normal.

## Water dripping indoors

If water drips from the indoor unit, the drain pipe is blocked or the unit is not level. Switch off the unit and call service; do not tilt the unit yourself.

## Noise

A hissing or gurgling sound when the unit starts or stops is refrigerant flowing and is normal. A rattling sound from the outdoor unit usually means a loose panel screw.
