# Paravi Warranty Policy

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## Warranty period

All Paravi appliances carry a 2-year warranty on parts and labour from the date on the invoice. The compressor of refrigerators and the inverter compressor of air conditioners carry a 10-year warranty on the part only; labour and gas refilling after the first 2 years are charged.

## What is not covered

- Damage caused by voltage fluctuation or lightning when the appliance was used without a recommended voltage stabilizer.
- Rust or corrosion on outdoor air conditioner units installed within 1 km of the sea, unless the anti-corrosion coating option was purchased.
- Damage caused by moving a washing machine without its transport bolts.
- Installation by anyone other than a Paravi authorised technician.
- Cosmetic damage, such as scratches or dents, reported more than 7 days after delivery.

## How to claim

Call the service hotline with the model number, serial number and invoice number. A technician visits within 3 working days in the Western Province and within 5 working days elsewhere. Keep the original invoice; a warranty claim without it is not accepted.

## Repairs and replacements

If the same fault is repaired three times within the warranty period and occurs a fourth time, the appliance is replaced with a new unit of the same or an equivalent model.
