# Paravi WF-70 Front Load Washing Machine — Owner's Manual (extract)

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## Load and detergent

Maximum load is 7 kg of dry cotton, 3.5 kg of synthetics and 2 kg of wool. Use low-foam detergent made for front loaders. Ordinary powder produces too much foam, which the machine detects and removes with extra rinses, making the cycle longer.

## Child lock

Press and hold TEMP and SPIN together for 3 seconds to lock or unlock the buttons. A key symbol shows on the display while the lock is on.

## Error codes

- E10: Water does not fill. Check that the tap is open and the inlet hose is not kinked. Clean the inlet filter at the back of the machine.
- E21: Water does not drain. Clean the drain pump filter behind the small door at the bottom right.
- UE: Unbalanced load. The machine cannot spin because the clothes have bunched to one side. Redistribute the load or add a few items.
- DE: Door not closed. Push the door firmly until it clicks.

## Drum clean

Run the Drum Clean programme once a month with the drum empty. It prevents bad smells and black mould on the door seal.

## Moving the machine

Before moving the machine to a new house, refit the three transport bolts at the back. Moving it without the bolts can damage the drum suspension, and this damage is not covered by the warranty.
