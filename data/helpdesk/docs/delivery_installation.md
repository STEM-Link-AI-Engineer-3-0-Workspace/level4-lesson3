# Delivery and Installation

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## Delivery

Delivery is free within 25 km of any Paravi showroom. Beyond 25 km, a charge of LKR 100 per km is made for the extra distance. Deliveries are made within 2 working days of purchase in the Western Province and within 4 working days elsewhere.

## Air conditioner installation

Standard installation is free with every air conditioner and includes up to 3 m of copper piping, a wall bracket for the outdoor unit, and the drain pipe. Extra piping is charged at LKR 3,500 per metre. Installation is completed within 48 hours of delivery.

## Washing machines and refrigerators

The delivery team unpacks, levels and tests washing machines and refrigerators. For washing machines, they remove the transport bolts and hand them to the customer; keep them for any future move.

## Old appliance collection

When you buy a new refrigerator or washing machine, the delivery team will take away your old appliance free of charge for recycling, if you request this at the time of purchase.
