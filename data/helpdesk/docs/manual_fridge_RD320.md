# Paravi RD-320 Double Door Refrigerator — Owner's Manual (extract)

Teaching corpus. Paravi Appliances is a fictional company.

## After installation

Wait 3 hours after the refrigerator has been moved or delivered before switching it on, so that the oil in the compressor settles. Allow 24 hours for it to reach full cold before loading fresh food.

## Temperature settings

Set the refrigerator compartment to 4 °C and the freezer to −18 °C for normal use. Set the freezer colder only when freezing a large quantity of fresh food, then return it to −18 °C.

## The refrigerator is not cooling

1. Check that the plug is in and the display is on.
2. Check that the door seals close fully; a sheet of paper closed in the door should not slide out easily.
3. Leave at least 10 cm of space behind and on each side of the refrigerator for air to circulate.
4. Do not place hot food inside; let it cool first.

If the freezer is cold but the lower compartment is warm, the air channel between them may be blocked by ice. Unplug the refrigerator for 12 hours with the doors open to defrost, then switch it on again.

## Door alarm

The alarm beeps if a door stays open for more than 2 minutes. Close the door to stop it.

## Power cuts

During a power cut, keep the doors closed. Food in a full freezer stays frozen for about 12 hours. Use a voltage stabilizer if the supply in your area fluctuates.
