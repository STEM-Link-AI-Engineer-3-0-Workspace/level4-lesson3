# Farm Record — Plot A-04, Ampara

Teaching corpus. This record is fictional.

Farmer: M. I. Faiz. Extent: 1.6 ha. Water source: Major irrigation scheme, left bank canal. Soil: Low humic gley, poorly drained.

## Maha 2024/25

Variety: Bg 403 (4 months), chosen for tolerance to brief flooding. Transplanted 2 November 2024.

Fertilizer: basal TSP 100 kg and MOP 48 kg. Urea 80 kg at 2 weeks, 120 kg at 5 weeks. Third top dressing postponed because of the flood.

Flood: Heavy rain from 9 to 12 January 2025. The field was submerged for 6 days. Water receded on 18 January. About 70 percent of the crop lodged and remained under water at the milky stage.

Claim: Damage reported to the Agrarian Service Centre on 20 January 2025. Claim reference CLM-2025-0412. Assessment visit on 3 February 2025. Assessed loss 80 percent. Compensation of LKR 160,000 approved for 1.6 ha and received on 28 March 2025.

Harvest: Salvage harvest of the standing area on 25 February 2025. Yield 0.9 t/ha.

## Yala 2025

Variety: At 307 (3 months), chosen because of iron toxicity symptoms on the low side of the field in previous seasons. Transplanted 20 April 2025.

Soil note: Orange-brown deposits seen in drains in May. Agricultural Instructor confirmed iron toxicity on the low side and advised improving drainage and adding MOP.

Harvest: 25 July 2025. Yield 5.6 t/ha.
