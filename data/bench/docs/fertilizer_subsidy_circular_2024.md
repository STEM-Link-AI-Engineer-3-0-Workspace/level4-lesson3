# Circular AGR/FS/2024/11 — Fertilizer Support for Paddy, Maha 2024/25

Teaching corpus. This circular is fictional and written for a training exercise.

Issued: 20 September 2024. Valid for the Maha 2024/25 season only.

## 1. Purpose

To support paddy farmers with the cost of fertilizer for the Maha 2024/25 season through a cash grant paid to the farmer's bank account.

## 2. Who is eligible

A farmer is eligible if all of the following apply:

- The farmer cultivates paddy in Maha 2024/25 on land registered in the paddy lands register of the Agrarian Service Centre.
- The cultivated extent is not more than 1 hectare. Land above 1 hectare is not covered.
- The farmer has a bank account in his or her own name.

Tenant cultivators are not eligible under this circular. Only the registered owner of the land may apply.

## 3. Amount of the grant

The grant is LKR 15,000 per hectare, paid for the cultivated extent up to the limit of 1 hectare. It is paid in a single instalment after the extent is verified.

## 4. How to apply

Submit the application form at the Agrarian Service Centre, together with a copy of the national identity card and the first page of the bank pass book. Applications for Maha 2024/25 close on 15 November 2024.

## 5. Verification

The Agrarian Development Officer verifies the cultivated extent in the field. False claims must be repaid in full.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this circular should be directed to the Agrarian Service Centre, quoting circular number AGR/FS/2024/11.
