# Farm Record — Plot A-11, Ampara

Teaching corpus. This record is fictional.

Farmer: S. Tharmalingam. Extent: 2.0 ha. Water source: Major irrigation scheme, right bank canal. Soil: Low humic gley.

## Maha 2024/25

Variety: Bg 94-1 (4 months). Transplanted 30 October 2024.

Fertilizer: basal TSP 124 kg, MOP 60 kg. Urea at 2 and 5 weeks; third and fourth top dressings not applied because of the flood.

Flood: The field was submerged from 10 to 16 January 2025 in the same flood that affected the neighbouring tract. About 60 percent of the crop lodged.

Claim: Damage reported to the Agrarian Service Centre on 2 February 2025, after the farmer returned from hospital. Claim reference CLM-2025-0398. Claim rejected because the damage was reported more than 14 days after it occurred. Appeal lodged on 20 February 2025; appeal not upheld.

Harvest: Salvage harvest on 22 February 2025. Yield 1.8 t/ha.

## Yala 2025

Variety: Bg 300 (3 months). Transplanted 25 April 2025.

Harvest: 30 July 2025. Yield 5.3 t/ha.

Note: The farmer organisation has asked the Agrarian Service Centre whether the 14-day rule can allow for illness. No reply has been recorded.
