# Farm Record — Plot P-30, Polonnaruwa

Teaching corpus. This record is fictional.

Farmer: D. P. Ajith Kumara. Extent: 0.6 ha. Water source: Major irrigation scheme. Soil: Non-calcic brown.

## Maha 2024/25

Variety: Bg 357 (3½ months). Sown 20 October 2024.

Fertilizer: urea applied at 2, 4, 6 and 7 weeks at full rates, plus an extra 25 kg at 8 weeks to improve the colour of the crop.

Lodging: Most of the crop lodged after strong wind on 15 January 2025, two weeks before harvest. Harvest by hand was slow and grain loss was high.

Disease: Neck blast seen on about 15 percent of panicles in early January. Not sprayed.

Harvest: 1 February 2025. Yield 3.9 t/ha.

Note from Agricultural Instructor: Bg 357 lodges under heavy nitrogen. Extra urea also favours blast. Follow the recommended schedule without additions.

## Yala 2025

Variety: Bg 352 (3½ months). Sown 3 April 2025. Fertilizer at recommended Yala rates, no extra urea.

Harvest: 18 July 2025. Yield 5.9 t/ha. No lodging.
