# Circular AGR/OFC/2025/03 — Fertilizer Support for Other Field Crops, Yala 2025

Teaching corpus. This circular is fictional and written for a training exercise.

Issued: 10 March 2025. Valid for the Yala 2025 season.

## 1. Purpose

To support farmers who grow other field crops, such as big onion, chilli, green gram, cowpea and soya bean, with the cost of fertilizer for the Yala 2025 season. Paddy is not covered by this circular.

## 2. Who is eligible

A farmer is eligible if the crop is grown on land registered with the Agrarian Service Centre, the cultivated extent is not more than 1 hectare, and the farmer has a bank account in his or her own name. Tenant cultivators may apply with a letter of consent from the landowner.

## 3. Amount of the grant

The grant is LKR 20,000 per hectare for chilli and big onion, and LKR 12,000 per hectare for green gram, cowpea and soya bean, paid for the cultivated extent up to 1 hectare. It is paid in a single instalment after the Agricultural Instructor confirms that the crop has been established.

## 4. How to apply

Submit the application form at the Agrarian Service Centre with a copy of the national identity card and the first page of the bank pass book. Applications for Yala 2025 close on 30 April 2025.

## 5. Contact

Questions should be directed to the Agrarian Service Centre, quoting circular number AGR/OFC/2025/03.
