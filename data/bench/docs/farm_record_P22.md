# Farm Record — Plot P-22, Polonnaruwa

Teaching corpus. This record is fictional.

Farmer: R. A. Nirmala Kumari. Extent: 2.4 ha, of which 2.0 ha is paddy and 0.4 ha is upland chilli. Water source: Major irrigation scheme. Soil: Non-calcic brown, alkaline in parts.

## Soil test, March 2025

Sample taken from the paddy area on 10 March 2025 and tested at the regional soil laboratory.

- pH 8.1 (alkaline).
- Available phosphorus 24 ppm (high).
- Exchangeable potassium adequate.
- Available zinc 0.4 ppm (deficient; adequate is above 1.0 ppm).

Laboratory advice: halve the TSP basal dose, apply zinc sulphate at 10 kg/ha, and do not increase urea to correct the yellowing seen in previous seasons.

## Yala 2025 — paddy

Variety: Bg 352 (3½ months). Sown 1 April 2025.

Fertilizer: basal TSP 60 kg for 2.0 ha (halved), MOP 60 kg, zinc sulphate 20 kg. Urea at 2, 4, 6 and 7 weeks, reduced by 10 percent for Yala.

Observation: No bronzing this season. Plants even in height.

Harvest: 20 July 2025. Yield 6.3 t/ha, against 4.8 t/ha in Yala 2024 when zinc was not applied.

## Yala 2025 — chilli

Variety: MI 2. Transplanted 15 April 2025 on 0.4 ha.

Pest log: 20 May 2025, leaves curling downward and bronzed underneath on about one fifth of plants. Sprayed sulphur on 21 May and again on 4 June. 12 June, a few plants with yellow, puckered leaves and severe stunting; removed and burnt.

Harvest: green chilli picked from 1 July to 15 August 2025. Total 3,100 kg.

## Maha 2025/26 — plans

The farmer intends to apply for the fertilizer grant for the 2.0 ha of paddy before the closing date.
