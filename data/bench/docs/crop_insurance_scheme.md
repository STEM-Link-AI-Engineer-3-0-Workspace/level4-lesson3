# Paddy Crop Protection Scheme — Rules for Farmers

Teaching corpus. This scheme is fictional and written for a training exercise.

## 1. What the scheme covers

The scheme compensates paddy farmers for crop loss caused by flood, drought, wild elephant damage, and outbreaks of pests or diseases that have been declared by the Department as an epidemic in the area. Loss caused by poor management, such as failure to apply fertilizer or to control weeds, is not covered.

## 2. Enrolment

Every farmer who receives the fertilizer grant for the season is enrolled automatically. Other farmers may enrol at the Agrarian Service Centre by paying a premium of LKR 1,000 per hectare before the end of the planting period.

## 3. Compensation

Compensation is paid only when the assessed loss is more than 50 percent of the expected yield. The maximum compensation is LKR 100,000 per hectare, for a maximum of 2 hectares per farmer per season. Where the loss is between 50 and 75 percent, half the maximum is paid; where it is above 75 percent, the full maximum is paid.

## 4. How to make a claim

Report the damage to the Agricultural Research and Production Assistant or the Agrarian Service Centre within 14 days of the damage. A claim reported after 14 days will be rejected, whatever the extent of the loss. Do not harvest or plough the damaged area until it has been inspected, because the assessor must see the damage in the field.

Each claim is given a reference number in the form CLM-year-number. Quote it in all later contact.

## 5. Assessment

An assessment committee of the Agricultural Instructor, the Agrarian Development Officer and a representative of the farmer organisation visits the field and estimates the loss as a percentage. Photographs taken by the farmer with the date visible are accepted as supporting evidence but do not replace the inspection.

## 6. Payment

Approved compensation is paid to the farmer's bank account within 60 days of the assessment. A farmer who disagrees with the assessment may appeal once, in writing, within 21 days of being informed of the result.

## 7. Wild elephant damage

For wild elephant damage, the farmer must also obtain a certificate from the Grama Niladhari confirming the incident. Fields beside an electric fence that the farmer organisation has failed to maintain are not covered for elephant damage.
