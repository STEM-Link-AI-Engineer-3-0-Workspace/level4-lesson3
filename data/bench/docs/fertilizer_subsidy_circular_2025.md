# Circular AGR/FS/2025/07 — Fertilizer Support for Paddy, Maha 2025/26

Teaching corpus. This circular is fictional and written for a training exercise.

Issued: 15 September 2025. This circular replaces Circular AGR/FS/2024/11 for the Maha 2025/26 season.

## 1. Purpose

To support paddy farmers with the cost of fertilizer for the Maha 2025/26 season through a cash grant paid to the farmer's bank account.

## 2. Who is eligible

A farmer is eligible if all of the following apply:

- The farmer cultivates paddy in Maha 2025/26 on land registered in the paddy lands register of the Agrarian Service Centre.
- The cultivated extent is not more than 2 hectares. Land above 2 hectares is not covered, even if it is registered.
- The farmer has a bank account in his or her own name.

Tenant cultivators are eligible for the land they cultivate, provided the landowner does not also claim for the same land.

## 3. Amount of the grant

The grant is LKR 25,000 per hectare, paid for the cultivated extent up to the limit of 2 hectares. A farmer cultivating 0.5 hectare receives LKR 12,500. The grant is paid in two equal instalments: the first after the extent is verified, and the second after the Agricultural Instructor confirms that the crop has been established.

## 4. How to apply

Submit the application form at the Agrarian Service Centre for the area where the land is situated, together with a copy of the national identity card and the first page of the bank pass book. Applications for Maha 2025/26 close on 31 October 2025. Late applications will not be accepted.

## 5. Verification

The Agrarian Development Officer verifies the cultivated extent in the field. A farmer who claims for more land than he or she cultivates will be required to repay the whole grant and will be excluded from the next two seasons.

## 6. Use of the grant

The grant is meant for urea, TSP and MOP. Farmers are encouraged to follow the fertilizer schedule for the age class of the variety they cultivate and to have their soil tested where a soil testing service is available.

## 7. Contact

Questions about this circular should be directed to the Agrarian Service Centre. The circular number AGR/FS/2025/07 must be quoted in all correspondence.
