# Farm Record — Plot K-17, Kurunegala

Teaching corpus. This record is fictional.

Farmer: W. M. Sunil Bandara. Extent: 0.8 ha. Water source: Minor tank, tail-end tract. Soil: Reddish brown earth, well drained.

## Yala 2024

Variety: Bg 300 (3 months), certified seed, blue tag, lot 24-117. Sown 2 April 2024, broadcast at 100 kg/ha.

Fertilizer applied: basal TSP 50 kg and MOP 24 kg on 1 April. Urea 40 kg on 16 April, 60 kg on 30 April. Third top dressing not applied; leaves were dark green at panicle initiation.

Water: Tank issue ended 28 June. Last two weeks of grain filling relied on rain. Some empty grains at the tail end of the plot.

Harvest: 4 July 2024. Yield 4.1 t/ha. Note from farmer: yield lower than the neighbours, probably water at the end.

## Maha 2024/25

Variety: Bg 360 (3½ months). Sown 18 October 2024.

Fertilizer: basal TSP 50 kg, MOP 24 kg. Urea at 2, 4, 6 and 7 weeks.

Pest log: 25 November 2024, brown planthopper found at the base of hills in the centre of the plot, about 20 per hill. Field drained for 4 days. 2 December, hopperburn patch about 6 m across in the centre. Sprayed buprofezin on 3 December. No further spread.

Harvest: 12 February 2025. Yield 5.2 t/ha, with loss in the hopperburn patch.

Note from Agricultural Instructor: Bg 360 is susceptible to BPH. For the next Maha, consider Bg 366 or start monitoring from week 4.

## Yala 2025

Variety: Bg 300. Sown 5 April 2025. AWD practised with a field water tube for the first time, irrigating at 15 cm below the surface.

Harvest: 8 July 2025. Yield 4.9 t/ha. Water issues used: 6, against 9 in Yala 2024.
