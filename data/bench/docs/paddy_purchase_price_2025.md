# Notice PMB/2025/04 — Guaranteed Purchase Price for Paddy, Maha 2025/26

Teaching corpus. This notice is fictional and written for a training exercise.

Issued: 1 October 2025.

## 1. Guaranteed prices

The state purchasing authority will buy paddy from registered farmers at the following guaranteed prices for the Maha 2025/26 harvest:

- Nadu (white or red): LKR 120 per kg.
- Samba: LKR 125 per kg.
- Keeri samba: LKR 132 per kg.

Prices apply to clean paddy at not more than 14 percent moisture. Paddy above 14 percent moisture is either refused or bought at a reduced price after a moisture deduction.

## 2. Limits

The authority will buy up to 2,000 kg per farmer per season at the guaranteed price. Paddy above this quantity may be sold to private buyers.

## 3. Where to sell

Paddy is bought at designated stores opened in each district during the harvest period. The list of stores and their opening dates is displayed at every Agrarian Service Centre. Bring the farmer registration card and the national identity card.

## 4. Quality

Paddy containing more than 2 percent of chaff, stones or other varieties will be refused. Paddy dried on roads is often refused because of stones and dirt.

## 5. Payment

Payment is made to the farmer's bank account within 7 working days of delivery.
