# Farm Record — Plot K-09, Kurunegala

Teaching corpus. This record is fictional.

Farmer: H. M. Chandrika Herath. Extent: 1.2 ha. Water source: Minor tank, head-end tract. Soil: Reddish brown earth.

## Yala 2024

Variety: Bg 352 (3½ months). Sown 28 March 2024.

Fertilizer: basal TSP 75 kg, MOP 36 kg. Urea at 2, 4, 6 and 7 weeks, reduced by 10 percent for Yala.

Weeds: Barnyard grass heavy on the high side of the field. Hand weeded at 3 weeks. Field to be levelled before Maha.

Harvest: 10 July 2024. Yield 5.8 t/ha.

## Maha 2024/25

Variety: Bg 366 (3½ months). Sown 12 October 2024.

Pest log: Leaf folder damage on about one third of the leaves in late November. Not sprayed; damage was before panicle initiation.

Harvest: 5 February 2025. Yield 6.4 t/ha.

## Yala 2025

Variety: Bg 300 (3 months). Sown 8 April 2025.

Weeds: Weedy rice seen for the first time, in patches near the inlet. Seed for this season was farmer-saved, not certified. Patches hand-pulled before flowering.

Harvest: 6 July 2025. Yield 5.1 t/ha. Paddy dried on tarpaulins to 14 percent moisture and sold to the purchasing authority.
